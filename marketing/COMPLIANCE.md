# Rules for promoting a token launch

This is research, not legal advice. Pooled launch participation and token
promotion can be regulated activities. Get the legal check from
`docs/GO-LIVE.md` before promoting to the public.

## Platform rules that apply everywhere
- **Use official APIs or schedulers built on them.** Every platform here bans
  browser bots, scrapers or self-bots on user accounts. X, Meta, YouTube,
  TikTok, Snapchat, Kick, Rumble, LinkedIn and Discord all say so explicitly.
  Breaking this gets accounts suspended.
- **Label automation.** X needs the "Automated" account label. Mastodon has an
  "automated account" box. Bluesky recommends a bot self-label.
- **No duplicate or mass posting.**
  - X bans identical or substantially similar posts, within one account or
    across accounts.
  - Reddit treats repeated self-promotion and cross-posting as spam.
  - Telegram and Discord ban unsolicited bulk messages.
- **No get-rich-quick framing.** Twitch, Snapchat, Pinterest and Brave ban it
  outright. Organic posts on most other platforms fall under scam/fraud rules.

## Paid ads, in short
- **Banned for a token launch:** Meta (ICOs banned outright), WhatsApp,
  Google/YouTube, Reddit, Pinterest, Snapchat (ICOs).
- **TikTok:** branded creator content about crypto is banned.
- **Licensed exchanges and wallets only, with pre-approval:** X, LinkedIn,
  Tumblr and TikTok ads.
- **Possible:** Brave Ads (memecoins in scope, with disclosures) and crypto ad
  networks.

## Influencers and paid posts
- **US FTC:** any material connection (payment, free tokens, family or work
  ties) must be disclosed clearly *in the post*, e.g. "#ad" or "Paid
  partnership". Brands should brief and monitor their promoters
  ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)).
- **X:** paid crypto partnerships reportedly allowed since March 2026 with the
  Paid Partnership label, but not in the EU, UK or Australia. Sources
  conflict, so this is unconfirmed.

## UK (FCA)
- **Who it covers:** crypto promotions that can reach UK consumers, including
  memes and finfluencer posts sent from abroad.
- **What they need:** approval by an FCA-authorised or registered firm, plus
  the prescribed risk warning: "Don't invest unless you're prepared to lose
  all the money you invest…"
- **Other rules:** a 24-hour cooling-off period for first-time investors, and
  a ban on referral bonuses.
- **Penalty:** an unlawful financial promotion is a **criminal offence**.
- **Coming up:** the new crypto authorisation regime opened its gateway on
  30 September 2026 and takes effect 25 October 2027.
- **Practical choice:** geo-exclude the UK from all promotion and from the
  dashboard, or get approval.
- Sources: [PS23/6](https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/policy/ps23-6.pdf),
  [FCA crypto marketing](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/cryptoassets/marketing-uk-consumers).

## EU (MiCA Article 7)
Marketing for crypto-assets must:
- be clearly identifiable as marketing
- be fair, clear and not misleading
- be consistent with a crypto-asset white paper and say where to find it
- name the offeror and give contact details
- say it hasn't been reviewed or approved by any competent authority

Whether a Bundlepad launch is an "offer to the public" (exemptions in
Article 4 may apply) needs a lawyer.
Source: [ESMA single rulebook, Art. 7](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-7-marketing-communications).

## Safe wording for Bundlepad posts
- **Say:** facts that can be checked:
  - "Commitments open until …"
  - "N wallets committed X SOL"
  - "Launch transaction: <explorer link>"
  - "Burned N $BUNDLEPAD: <tx>"
  - the fee (10%), and that every step is on the public integrity chain
- **Don't say:**
  - price predictions
  - "guaranteed", "100% profitable", "can't lose"
  - "get in early to 10x"
  - anything implying the platform washes, mixes or hides funds
- **Always:** include a risk line ("Memecoins are extremely risky; only commit
  what you can afford to lose"). Disclose any payment you made to a promoter.
